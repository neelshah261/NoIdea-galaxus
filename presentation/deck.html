Six questions for every product image

Hack Apertus Zurich · Digitec GalaxusThe problem

Three traps in the score

Rare classes count

Macro F1 weighs a rare class as much as a common one, so answering the usual class is punished.

Context misleads

With the catalog context, the 8B scores {EXP-0002.macro_f1.subject} on subject, the same as always answering the majority class ({EXP-0001.macro_f1.subject}). Without it: {EXP-0009.macro_f1.subject}. And in production the context can be missing.

Left is not right

Perspective is the business priority and earns a bonus. The 8B zero-shot starts at {EXP-0002.macro_f1.perspective} macro F1, and left/right pairs are the hosts' own worst confusions.

Macro F1 on our official holdout ({EXP-0002.holdout.images} train images, {EXP-0002.holdout.products} products), results/metrics.json.
